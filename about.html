Who We Are

Leading Service Provider since 1998

Founded in 1998 by Hingurangalage Don Ananda, A.R.G Distributors PVT LTD is one of the largest hardware manufacturers and distributors in Sri Lanka. We are proud distributors of JAT Holding PLC, INSEE Mahaweli Marine Cement, IDEA Metals Limited, NEMSU PVC, ANANDA PVC, RAPID Enterprises & Dealers of Many Well-known Hardware Brands in Sri Lanka with a great 25 Years Of Customer Satisfaction.
